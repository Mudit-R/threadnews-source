# Original source summaries

The Worker checks for eligible headline-only and excerpt records after the existing ten-minute news refresh. It leases up to three rows per run, retrying transient problems later, and limits generation to 30 attempts per UTC day across scheduled and manual runs. It doesn't need a visitor to open the story. `GET /api/pipeline/status` reports the latest scheduled run; `POST /api/admin/refresh-source-summaries` runs one small batch using the existing ingestion authorization.

`backend/src/enrichment.js` keeps policies scoped to verified publishers and document paths. The reader checks robots rules before each fetch, bounds redirects and document size, and extracts body paragraphs rather than search or social previews. It skips paywalls and indexing restrictions. When extending the registry, confirm automated access and the permitted use of article text, check the publisher's claims and attribution requirements, and verify the body extraction on current releases.

The generator sees a small sample of paragraphs, not credentials or private user data. Summaries are limited to 40 words and checked for cited evidence, numbers present in that evidence, meaningful overlap with the body beyond the headline, and long copied phrases. These checks do not independently confirm every claim. D1 stores the short summary and source provenance, never the original body. The Worker saves a revision and does not overwrite an editorial summary or a record changed during the run. Future feed copies cannot replace the saved summary with a headline.

The article shows **Summary** without a click. A headline-only story is labelled as such until an original can be read. Google News discovery links often do not provide a public direct publisher URL; the app does not use private resolver endpoints or try to bypass paywalls. For those records, an editor can verify the publisher link and publish an attributed summary through the regular ingestion path.
